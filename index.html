How to play

Combine the six figures with +, −, × and ÷ to hit the amount due exactly. Pick a number, an operation, then a second number — the result is pencilled in as a new figure you can spend. Each figure may be spent once. Subtraction must stay positive and division must come out even: this ledger holds only positive whole numbers. A fresh account opens at midnight.
