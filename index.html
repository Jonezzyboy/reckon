How to play
  1. Pick a figure, then an operation (+ − × ÷), then a second figure.

  3. Hit the amount due exactly to earn the Settled stamp.
  • Each figure may be spent only once.
  • Subtraction must stay positive, division must come out even — this ledger holds only positive whole numbers.
